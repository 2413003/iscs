Make a claim

You only need to claim if the firm you invested with has stopped trading and cannot return your money. There is no charge to claim.

  1. Check you are eligibleConfirm the firm is covered and that your investment is a protected type.
  2. Send your detailsComplete the claim form and upload proof of identity and your account statements.
  3. We review and payWe assess your claim and pay eligible compensation directly to you.

Firm guidance

Be careful of scams

ISCS will never ask you to pay a fee to receive compensation, and will never ask for your full password or PIN.

If someone contacts you unexpectedly claiming to be from ISCS, end the call and contact us using the details on this page.
